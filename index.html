Campus Navigation FAQs

Quick answers for freshers & visitors

How do I read LPU room numbers (e.g., 34-402)?

The number before the hyphen is the Block Number (Block 34). The first digit after the hyphen is the Floor (4th floor), and the remaining digits indicate the room or lab number.

How do I use this map without scrolling dropdowns?

Click any blue circle directly on the map and tap Set Start or Set Destination inside the popup to plot your route immediately.

When should I walk vs. take an e-rickshaw?

If your estimated distance is under 600 meters (about 7–8 minutes), walking is usually faster than waiting in queue for an e-rickshaw between lectures.

Why does "My Location" say I'm outside campus?

To keep the map focused on LPU, live GPS routing only activates inside the campus boundary. Make sure your device's high-accuracy location permission is enabled.

Where do I go for ID cards, attendance, or admin queries?

Head to Blocks 29–32 near the Main Gate Plaza for admissions, fee counters, and administrative helpdesks, or Block 13 for Student Welfare (DSW).

How are walking routes calculated?

The navigator queries OpenStreetMap pedestrian pathways to follow actual campus roads and walkways rather than cutting through buildings.
